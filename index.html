About me

I’m a visual artist focused on animation and motion design. I mix new tools with traditional craft to create visuals that feel fresh, distinctive, and built with intention.

Over the years, I’ve worked across music, advertising, and independent projects. I’ve created projection mapped visuals, branded content, and fully animated music videos, often taking things from the first idea all the way to final delivery. I like pushing concepts past the obvious and finding the version that feels bold and clear. Sometimes that means building experimental looks in ComfyUI, sometimes it is character work in Toon Boom, and sometimes it is shaping the final polish in After Effects and DaVinci.

At the core, I care about storytelling and imagination. I love taking a simple concept and turning it into something that feels alive, something that pulls people in and makes them stop for a moment.

I’m always open to new collaborations with brands, agencies, and other creatives. If you have a project you want to bring to life, I’d love to connect.
