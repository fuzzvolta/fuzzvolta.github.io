About

I'm Diego Salas, a director and editor working across ads, animation and AI video. Diegomotion is the studio name I've worked under for more than a decade, making brand content, music videos and live visuals for brands and artists in Peru and abroad.

Most recently I edited the Spanish line of direct-response ads at Ancient Remedies, where I also built the AI footage pipeline the editor team adopted.

I start with the idea and pick the tools after. A project might begin in ComfyUI or Blender and end in After Effects or DaVinci Resolve. The goal doesn't change: a simple story that makes people stop.

Based in Lima, often in Madrid. I work remotely in English and Spanish.
