About

I'm a video editor and motion designer working on performance ads, animation and music videos. I spent the last stretch as the Spanish-line editor at Bublishing, shipping direct-response ads at volume and building the AI footage pipeline the team now runs on. Before that: projection-mapped live visuals, branded content and fully animated music videos, usually from first idea to final delivery.

I mix new tools with traditional craft. Sometimes that means an experimental look built in ComfyUI, sometimes character work in Toon Boom or Cinema 4D, sometimes the final polish in After Effects and DaVinci. What stays constant is storytelling: a simple idea turned into something that makes people stop.

Based in Lima, regularly in Madrid, working remotely with teams in the US and Europe. Open to full-time and contract roles in performance creative, motion and AI video.
